## Working recommendation

**Continental DWS 06 Plus** for balanced wet performance, comfort and value; **Falken FK460** when minimizing purchase cost is most important.

**First local bids:** Tire Rack shipped to Firestone in Jersey City; BJ's Jersey City; Costco Bayonne; Mavis North Bergen. Compare the *complete* bill including tires, mounting, balancing, disposal, NJ sales tax, tire fee, TPMS and extra shop fees, less **confirmed** discounts.

**Before placing order:** verify exact tire sidewall sizes and vehicle compatibility. Expected 20-inch staggered: 245/35ZR20 95Y XL front, 275/30ZR20 97Y XL rear. These specifications are manufacturer directory candidates, not a direct inspection of the vehicle.
