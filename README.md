### Source quality and quote hygiene

Retail numbers in this repository are **dated research snapshots only**, not firm offers or complete installed totals. Call the vendor and capture the actual quote in the tracker. Confirm the same model is available in both staggered sizes. Do not assume headline treadwear warranties cover staggered fitments, or that mounted/driven tires are fully refundable.
