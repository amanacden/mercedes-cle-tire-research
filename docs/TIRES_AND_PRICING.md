## Important policy cautions

- **Tire Rack**: ordinary return eligibility is generally for **unused, undriven** tires. Confirm eligibility and any exceptions directly. Not an automatic 30-day installed road-test refund.
- **Costco**: road-hazard terms can involve proration, specific coverage limits and Costco installation conditions. Confirm exact warehouse and size availability.
- **Michelin**: inspect eligibility and remedy terms for any 60-day satisfaction program; do **not** assume full cash refund in every circumstance.
- **Mileage warranties**: staggered applications can have reduced or excluded treadwear coverage; ask for the exact warranty PDF and service terms.
- **Promos/rebates**: list *conditional* rebates in notes; do not subtract them from confirmed net cost until eligibility is proven.

## Formula: compare out-the-door numbers

```
Net installed = (2 × front tire) + (2 × rear tire)
              + shipping + mount/balance + TPMS/valves + disposal
              + sales tax + required fees
              + optional alignment (show separately)
              - confirmed rebates - confirmed selling-dealer credit
```
