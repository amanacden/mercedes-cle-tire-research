Could you please email the signed CPO certification inspection for my vehicle, including all four tire readings at certification and sale, the tire make/model and DOT age recorded, any tire-and-wheel protection purchased, and the tire tread-depth/condition standards in force on the sale date?

Preliminary sizes are **2 × 245/35ZR20 95Y XL front** and **2 × 275/30ZR20 97Y XL rear**, subject to final sidewall/placard confirmation.

Please provide exact tire model/SKU, stock, earliest installation date, tire-only subtotal, shipping, mounting, balancing, TPMS/valves, disposal, taxes, required fees and final out-the-door total. Please identify rebate rules and optional alignment and road-hazard costs separately.

Also confirm that you accept this fitment, and state the refund/exchange policy **after mounting/driving** (not merely unused returns). I am comparing Continental DWS 06 Plus, Michelin Pilot Sport All Season 4, and equivalent value alternatives. Thank you.
