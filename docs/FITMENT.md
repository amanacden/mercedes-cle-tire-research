## Confirm in writing

- [ ] Factory VIN-specific front/rear sizing, minimum load/speed, correct XL rating.
- [ ] Existing tires' **MO**, **MOE**, run-flat or other sidewall markings; confirm whether an OE approval or emergency inflator kit matters for any replacement.
- [ ] Same make/model at both axles, correct front/rear rolling circumference and 4MATIC requirements.
- [ ] Performance all-season suitability for planned driving in New Jersey. All-season does not mean equivalent to winter tires in severe snow/ice.
- [ ] Whether repair versus replacing 2/4 tires is acceptable; check difference in tread depths before any 4MATIC mixing.
- [ ] Whether alignment is justified by uneven tread wear, pulling, repair history or installer findings.
- [ ] Tire/wheel protection, road-hazard or manufacturer warranties already on the car.
- [ ] Actual CPO tire report dated around Sep 13, 2026 and the precise standards then applicable.

## CPO inspection — evidence rather than assumptions

At purchase the tires were reportedly around **5/32**. Some historic Mercedes CPO inspection material cites 5/32 as a minimum; it is **not sufficient to prove the rule for this sale**, nor that seller owes a replacement. Request the **actual signed 2026 CPO checklist**, separate measurements for all tires, any 2026 minimum, and a written response on possible assistance.
