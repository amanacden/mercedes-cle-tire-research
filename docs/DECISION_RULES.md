# Financial decision rules — buy, negotiate, defer, or trade

## Decide whether four new tires are actually the best use of cash

1. **Near-term sale/trade?** Ask the same dealer/buyer for an as-is offer and an offer assuming a new compatible matching 4-tire set. If the incremental trade value is well below the installed tire cost, replacing immediately may be a net loss *unless condition requires it*.
2. **Keeping for NJ winter?** Verify tire condition, existing tire season/type and tread. Evaluate suitable all-season options by wet and cold-weather performance as well as price.
3. **Selling dealer will help?** Subtract only **written, confirmed** Scottsdale assistance and any applicable conditions. Don't assume CPO covers tire wear.
4. **Fitment not verified?** No purchase. Confirm sizes/load/speed, 4MATIC pairing, run-flat/MO requirements and the shop's willingness to install.

## Illustrative economics (not a retailer quote)

## Keep-the-car weighted scoring, after fitment gating

| Criterion | Weight | Evaluation |
|---|---:|---|
| All-in installed price, net of verified rebates | 35 | Complete written out-the-door quote |
| Wet/cold-weather traction | 25 | Independent comparative test evidence |
| Noise and ride comfort | 15 | Real-world/independent testing; relevant to 20-inch rims |
| Actual warranty and road-hazard eligibility | 10 | Staggered adjustment, terms, proration |
| Installer skill and rim/TPMS handling | 10 | Written guarantee/equipment |
| Stock and installation lead time | 5 | Local availability |

If trading, use **incremental trade-in value versus true tire spending** instead of a keep-car performance score.

## Avoidable money mistakes

- Do not confuse rear 275/**30**R20 with 275/**35**R20.
- Do not compare website tire-only totals against dealer out-the-door numbers.
- Do not treat a conditional rebate as money already saved.
- Do not assume all retail tire trials permit used/mounted returns.
- Do not assume the headline mileage warranty applies unchanged to staggered cars.
- Do not buy four new tires before checking whether the car will be traded shortly.
