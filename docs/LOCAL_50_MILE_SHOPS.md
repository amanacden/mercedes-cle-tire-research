**Constraint added 2026-11-01:** For this CLE tire project, consider **only tire purchases that result in installation at a shop or mobile service no more than 50 driving miles from the user's home in Jersey City Heights, NJ**. The home street address is deliberately not stored in this public repository. In calculating travel distance, use the exact home origin privately in a directions tool **before booking**. Do not confuse approximate retailer-directory distances, straight-line miles, or ZIP-to-ZIP distance with actual driving miles.

**Current shortlist is intentionally much closer than 50 miles** (Jersey City, Bayonne, North Bergen, Newark, and Paramus). In all cases obtain *written* tire inventory, fitment acceptance, installed total, and appointment availability. These are verified business locations/installation services, **not guaranteed tire stock or current installed quotes**. Exclude any further shop if actual car route exceeds 50 miles; prioritize NJ over toll bridges unless final net savings justify tolls.

## Five questions to ask every candidate

1. Can you supply or install **2 × 245/35ZR20 95Y XL front and 2 × 275/30ZR20 97Y XL rear** (pending verification against physical tire sidewalls and placard)? Same tire model front/rear?
2. Can you confirm the **CLE 300 4MATIC Cabriolet, 20-inch AMG wheels** is acceptable, including all load/speed, non-OE/standard-vs-runflat and 4MATIC limits? Need written acceptance.
3. What's the **all-in price**, with tires, fitting, balancing, TPMS, disposal, tax, membership, shipping and any other fees? Itemize optional alignment/road force.
4. What is the **earliest installation date**, and are both sizes in stock / orderable?
5. How are rims protected, what is the written wheel damage policy, and what are mounted/used return or exchange rules?
